01 · Your briefNine problems you named, and what changes

We started from your process, not from software menus. Every line below comes from your ERP and CRM requirements of 3 November 2026 and has an acceptance test we will run with you.

What you face todayWhat the platform does
Emails, attachments and WhatsApp information are scattered.One searchable case per RFQ with every email, file, decision and its full history.
The same details are copied into trackers, supplier requests, quotations and orders.AI reads each RFQ once, from email, Excel, Word, PDF or a photo. The data flows to every later step without retyping.
Ownership and next actions can be unclear, especially at handover.Every live case has a named owner, a backup, a deadline and a next action. Reassignments are logged.
Sourcing is repeated because past suppliers and prices are hard to find.Your inbox history is organised into customers, vessels, suppliers and dated prices, even where no order was placed.
Offers may have wrong specifications, brands, quantities, units or pack sizes.Every offer line is compared with the original request. Deviations are flagged and resolved before use.
Unanswered supplier requests and customer quotations are tracked by hand.Separate waiting states, scheduled reminders, overdue alerts and stop rules. Replies are matched even when the subject changes.
Old quotations and changed supplier prices can lead to losses.Quotation versions, current cost checks, approved FX and margin checks before any commitment.
Delivery, partial supplies, invoices and payments are followed separately.Linked purchasing, delivery, billing and collection on the same case, with outstanding balances visible.
Management lacks one live view of progress and profitability.Live dashboards for blockers, workload, conversion, cash exposure and actual profit, plus a 07:00 daily briefing.

Three principles

01The RFQ stays the parent case

Lines can be sourced, delivered and invoiced in stages. A partly quoted or partly delivered case never shows as complete.

02AI reads, people decide

AI extracts, matches and drafts. Prices, equivalent parts, approvals and payments always need a named person.

03Proven before paid

Acceptance tests from your brief for every requirement. The last 20% of each phase is due after 30 days of real use.
